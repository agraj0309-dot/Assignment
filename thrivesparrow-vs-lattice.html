The ThriveSparrow angle

One loop from signal → action → growth.

Instead of treating engagement, goals, performance and recognition as separate programs, position them as connected moments in the employee journey.

PMM thesis: The value is not “more features.” It is reducing the distance between what employees say, what managers see, and what the organization does next.
